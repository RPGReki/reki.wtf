Interpretation 2 (Tian)

pin comes from the circular shape of barn (place to store rice or other food). It means people have plenty of food to eat and do not suffer from hunger.

1sou shapes like bird and it is sparrow, who eats the food in barn. Sou is counting how many sparrow caught by people. People use a bamboo made, rectangular shape counter to count

man is a counting unit which equals ten thousand. People could exchange one sparrow for 1 man money at the government.

hatsu “發” means earning a great fortune with business or getting straight promotion in government place

chun “中” means hitting the target in an important exam (E.g. gets a good grade in College Entrance Examination and enters the dream school)

haku “白” is a tool of high official used in ancient China
